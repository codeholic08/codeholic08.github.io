I'm a Software Engineer who came up through backend systems and moved toward AI infrastructure. Two years at Barclays building services and terabyte-scale data pipelines, an ML internship at Qualcomm on RAG and multi-agent systems, and an M.S. in Computer Science at NYU, where I now do vector search research.

The thread through all of it is that I don't like working at one layer. At Barclays I was writing FastAPI services and also managing the Spark and AWS pipelines feeding them. At Qualcomm I built the agent system and the evaluation harness that judged it. I'd rather understand the whole path from data to production than own one well-defined piece of it, and that's the kind of team I'm looking for.

At NYU Courant, I am developing VecFast, a high-update-rate vector database with a two-tier ANN index — an in-memory HNSW hot tier absorbing streaming inserts and a cluster-based cold tier, merged by an adaptive centroid algorithm that detects distribution shift — and benchmarking recall-latency tradeoffs against FAISS and HNSW baselines on NYU HPC.

At Qualcomm, I fine-tuned Qwen and integrated a retrieval layer into a modem-framework QA pipeline, cutting manual validation time by 65% across 9 test suites, and built an LLM evaluation workflow that reduced operating cost by 30%. I also architected a multi-agent orchestration layer coordinating six specialized workers with confidence-based intent routing, reaching 99% task completion, and engineered a distributed telemetry pipeline on AWS EMR and Hadoop that cut analysis turnaround by 35%.

Previously at Barclays, I fine-tuned FinBERT and Instructor-XL for financial-document classification and retrieval, achieving 0.98 F1, and integrated ChromaDB-backed semantic search into a platform credited with eight-figure business impact. I built LLM-powered chatbots, entity extraction, and summarization tools that reduced manual workload by 45%, automated performance evaluation across 25,000+ customer records, and engineered a FastAPI backend for a batch-pricing tool.

Beyond engineering, I serve as VP of Communications for the NYU Graduate Student Council (6,000+ students), previously served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant for Computer Systems Organization at NYU, and am a recipient of the Barclays World Class Customer Service Award.
